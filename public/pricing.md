# AdFunnl pricing

AdFunnl is a marketing analytics and ad reporting platform for agencies, media buyers and performance marketers. Prices are per month, in USD or EGP. Annual billing costs ten months for twelve, so two months are free. Every paid plan starts with a full-access free trial that falls back to the Free plan when it ends.

Price change: the prices below apply until 31 October 2026. They go up on 1 November 2026. A subscription or renewal bought before then keeps the current price for the period bought.

Sign up: https://app.adfunnl.com/signup
Full pricing page: https://www.adfunnl.com/pricing

## Free

- Price: $0 / EGP 0 per month
- For: trying it with one brand
- Limits: 1 team seat, 1 brand, 3 ad accounts, 2 ad platforms, 3 dashboards
- Data refresh: every 12 hours
- History: 3 months
- Includes: creative calculator

## Solo

- Price: $19 / EGP 990 per month ($190 / EGP 9,900 per year)
- For: freelancers and single operators
- Limits: 1 team seat, 3 brands, 15 ad accounts, all ad platforms, unlimited dashboards
- Data refresh: every 12 hours
- History: 1 year
- Includes: everything in Free, plus change annotations, Hook & Hold, Creative Fatigue, live sharing with view-only links and PDF, add-fund reminders, billing alerts and Google Sheets import

## Growth

- Price: $49 / EGP 2,490 per month ($490 / EGP 24,900 per year)
- For: small teams that want the AI assistant
- Limits: 3 team seats, 10 brands, 50 ad accounts, all ad platforms, unlimited dashboards
- Data refresh: every 6 hours
- History: 3 years
- Includes: everything in Solo, plus the AI assistant, AI-built dashboards, MCP access, ad control (changing your ads from Claude or ChatGPT) and 1,000 AI credits per month

## Agency

- Price: $95 / EGP 4,990 per month ($950 / EGP 49,900 per year)
- For: agencies
- Limits: 10 team seats, 30 brands, 150 ad accounts, all ad platforms, unlimited dashboards
- Data refresh: hourly
- History: 5 years
- Includes: everything in Growth, plus 3,000 AI credits per month

## Add-ons (billed monthly)

- AI add-on: $8 / EGP 390 per month. The AI assistant with 300 credits a month, and MCP access so Claude or ChatGPT can read your AdFunnl data.
- Ad control: $10 / EGP 500 per month, on its own, no AI add-on needed. Claude or ChatGPT can change your ads after you approve each change. Details: https://www.adfunnl.com/ai-ad-control

- Extra seat: $8 / EGP 400 per seat per month, on Solo and Growth. Adds one more teammate who can sign in and work on your brands. Solo includes 1 seat and Growth includes 3. Agency includes 10 seats; beyond that is Enterprise.

Growth and Agency include the AI add-on and ad control. Add-ons are requested and approved by the AdFunnl team. Ad control is not part of the free trial.

## AI credit top-ups (Growth and Agency)

- 500 credits: $9 / EGP 490
- 1,500 credits: $25 / EGP 1,290
- 5,000 credits: $65 / EGP 3,490

Monthly credits carry over, and top-up credits are only spent after the monthly allowance runs out.

## Billing

Paid plans are enabled after payment is confirmed. You request a plan and a person approves it, so there is no instant self-checkout. Payment is accepted in EGP and USD.

Questions: support@adfunnl.com
