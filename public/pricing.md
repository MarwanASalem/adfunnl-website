# AdFunnl pricing

AdFunnl is a marketing analytics and ad reporting platform for agencies, media buyers and performance marketers. Prices are per month, in USD or EGP. Annual billing costs ten months for twelve, so two months are free. Every paid plan starts with a full-access free trial that falls back to the Free plan when it ends.

Sign up: https://app.adfunnl.com/signup
Full pricing page: https://www.adfunnl.com/pricing

## Free

- Price: $0 / EGP 0 per month
- For: trying it with one brand
- Limits: 1 team seat, 1 brand, 3 ad accounts, 2 ad platforms, 3 dashboards
- Data refresh: every 12 hours
- History: 3 months
- Includes: creative calculator

## Solo

- Price: $25 / EGP 1,490 per month ($250 / EGP 14,900 per year)
- For: freelancers and single operators
- Limits: 1 team seat, 3 brands, 15 ad accounts, all ad platforms, unlimited dashboards
- Data refresh: every 12 hours
- History: 1 year
- Includes: everything in Free, plus change annotations, Hook & Hold, Creative Fatigue, live sharing with view-only links and PDF, add-fund reminders, billing alerts, Google Sheets import, the AI assistant, AI-built dashboards, MCP access, ad control (changing your ads from Claude or ChatGPT) and 300 AI credits per month

## Growth

- Price: $49 / EGP 2,990 per month ($490 / EGP 29,900 per year)
- For: small teams
- Limits: 3 team seats, 10 brands, 50 ad accounts, all ad platforms, unlimited dashboards
- Data refresh: every 6 hours
- History: 3 years
- Includes: everything in Solo, plus 1,000 AI credits per month

## Agency

- Price: $99 / EGP 5,990 per month ($990 / EGP 59,900 per year)
- For: agencies
- Limits: 10 team seats, 30 brands, 150 ad accounts, all ad platforms, unlimited dashboards
- Data refresh: hourly
- History: 5 years
- Includes: everything in Growth, plus 2,000 AI credits per month

## Add-ons (billed monthly)

- Extra seat: $8 / EGP 490 per seat per month, on Solo and Growth. Adds one more teammate who can sign in and work on your brands. Solo includes 1 seat and Growth includes 3. Agency includes 10 seats; beyond that is Enterprise.

Every paid plan includes the AI assistant and ad control: Claude or ChatGPT can change your ads after you approve each change (details: https://www.adfunnl.com/ai-ad-control). Add-ons are requested and approved by the AdFunnl team. Ad control is not part of the free trial.

## AI credit top-ups (every paid plan)

- 500 credits: $12 / EGP 690
- 1,500 credits: $39 / EGP 2,390
- 5,000 credits: $129 / EGP 7,790

Monthly credits carry over, and top-up credits are only spent after the monthly allowance runs out.

## Billing

Paid plans are enabled after payment is confirmed. You request a plan and a person approves it, so there is no instant self-checkout. Payment is accepted in EGP and USD.

Questions: support@adfunnl.com
